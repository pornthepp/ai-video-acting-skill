# Cinematic AI Video Acting Prompt Recipes

A curated library of ready-to-use, Oscar-grade acting prompt recipes organized by dramatic genre and character intention.

## 1. Psychological Thriller & Interrogation

### Scene: The Cold Deflection (To conceal / To deflect)
* **Objective / Verb**: To deflect suspicion without breaking composure.
* **Format**: Text-to-Video (T2V)

```text
A composed corporate executive sits opposite an unseen interrogator, hands folded flat on the polished table, already anchored in a quiet upright posture.

She keeps her eyes fixed on the interviewer off-camera left. On a shallow inhale, her lower eyelids tighten slightly while her lips remain pressed together in a neutral line. She swallows once, lets her gaze shift briefly down to her watch for half a second, then locks eye contact again as she exhales quietly through her nose.

Locked 85mm medium close-up, sharp eye focus, shallow depth of field. Cold fluorescent side light creates subtle falloff on one cheek. Restrained, unreadable natural performance.
```

## 2. Intimate Drama & Suppressed Grief

### Scene: The Unread Message (To swallow defeat / To protect from seeing pain)
* **Objective / Verb**: To protect someone from seeing that they hurt you.
* **Format**: Image-to-Video (I2V)

```text
The subject holds the coffee cup steady with both hands.
Her eyes remain fixed on the window glass, then shift slowly toward the doorway.
On a faint inhale, the corner of her lower lip tenses for a beat; she blinks once, held fractionally longer than usual.
She releases her jaw on a slow nasal exhale and lowers her gaze to the table.
Locked telephoto close-up with a barely perceptible slow push-in.
Morning window light catches the moisture line along her lower eyelid.
```

## 3. Neo-Noir & Hardboiled Investigation

### Scene: The Matchbook Clue (To assess the threat / To corner)
* **Objective / Verb**: To trap the witness into continuing their lie.
* **Format**: Kling AI (T2V)

```text
A tired homicide detective sits motionless behind a scarred wooden desk, his body angled toward an unseen witness off-camera right.
Subject movement: He rolls a closed matchbook once between his fingers and stops. His eyes remain locked on the witness. His lower eyelids tighten slightly as he exhales through his nose, then he lowers his gaze to the matchbook.
Scene: Dim interrogation room with Venetian blinds.
Camera: Tight 85mm close-up, locked frame with very subtle final push-in.
Lighting: Slanted Venetian-blind tungsten light cutting across one eye.
Atmosphere: Smoky, tense, quiet realism.
```

## 4. Sci-Fi & Psychological Isolation

### Scene: The Ghost Frequency (To verify / To resist panic)
* **Objective / Verb**: To test if what she heard was real while resisting emotional breakdown.
* **Format**: MiniMax / Hailuo

```text
[Static shot]
A solitary spacecraft navigator stands before the glowing console, her hand frozen two centimeters above the audio frequency knob.
Her eyes remain locked on the audio waveform display, then dart once toward the empty cockpit chair beside her without moving her head.
A shallow inhale catches in her throat; her lower eyelids tighten and her lips part slightly.
She swallows once, exhales slowly, and lowers her hand to rest against the console edge.
Cool cyan instrument light reflects across her cheek with faint specular highlights in the eyes.
```

## 5. Period Romance & Unspoken Longing

### Scene: The Parting Glance (To hold them here / To surrender)
* **Objective / Verb**: To tempt them to stay without speaking a word.
* **Format**: Runway / Sora

```text
Action beats:
- She stands by the open carriage door in the fading twilight, holding her parasol against her shoulder in stillness.
- As the footsteps stop, her eyes lift slowly from the gravel path to meet his eyes just off-camera right.
- One corner of her mouth begins to soften but stops before forming a smile; a quiet breath parts her lips.
- She holds the gaze for two beats, blinks once gently, and turns her head toward the road on the exhale.

Camera: Locked 70mm medium close-up, soft dusk backlighting with warm rim along the hair and silhouette.
Performance: Restrained period-drama naturalism, real-time micro-acting.
```

## 6. Cyberpunk & Synthetic Restraint

### Scene: The Identity Scan (To conceal / To pass inspection)
* **Objective / Verb**: To conceal synthetic identity under scrutiny without triggering alarm.
* **Format**: Seedance 2.5 / Jimeng (T2V / I2V)

```text
Spatial Anchor: A young synthetic courier stands at a neon-lit security checkpoint, already holding an identity card beneath the red laser scanner, posture calm and upright.

Character Kinematics:
- Beat 1 (0–1.5s): The scanner beeps an unexpected code. Her thumb pauses against the edge of the card.
- Beat 2 (1.5–3.5s): Her eyes shift from the scanner beam toward the security guard off-camera right without moving her head. A subtle breath catches; her lower eyelids tighten, then her jaw loosens.
- Beat 3 (3.5–5.0s): She blinks once, exhales softly through her nose, and returns her gaze down to the scanner plate as her shoulders settle.

Camera Trajectory: 85mm medium close-up, locked composition with a barely perceptible slow push-in.
Lighting & Atmosphere: Crisp red laser line sweeping across cheekbone, cold cyan overhead LED fill, atmospheric rain reflections.
Style: Cinematic photorealism, restrained realistic micro-performance, natural muscle dynamics.
```
