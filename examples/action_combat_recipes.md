# Cinematic Action & Combat Prompt Recipes

A curated collection of production-ready, 3-shot action sequences designed to bypass AI multi-body collision flaws, limb morphing, and floaty physics using **Shot Decomposition**, **State-Transition Prompting**, and **Environmental FX Masking**.

---

## 1. Rain-Soaked Alley Fight & Wall Takedown (Hong Kong / 87Eleven Style)

### Shot 1: The Guard & Tension (1.5s) — Setup Beat
* **Focus**: Base stance, weight distribution, intense eye-lock, breath.
* **Format**: Seedance 2.5 / Kling (T2V / I2V)

```text
Tight medium 35mm, rainy neon alley. An 18yo athletic woman backed against a wet brick wall, breathing heavily through clenched teeth. Her weight sinks firmly onto her rear foot, fists held in a tight high guard. Her eyes lock aggressively off-camera right without blinking, rain streaming down her jaw and knuckles. Locked dynamic camera, shallow depth of field, neon magenta reflections across wet skin.
```

### Shot 2: The Block & Shoulder Drive (1.2s) — Strike & Collision Beat
* **Focus**: Single explosive forward momentum, shoulder-first impact into wall, environmental rain splash.
* **Format**: Hailuo / Runway / Seedance 2.5

```text
[Push in] Fast handheld whip-pan. The athletic woman steps forward explosively, driving her shoulder hard into a larger man's chest and forcing him backward into the rain-slick brick wall. Heavy water and rain spray bursts off the brick wall upon impact. High kinetic energy, realistic body weight and resistance, motion blur, shutter at 45 degrees.
```

### Shot 3: The Recoil & Standoff (2.0s) — Reaction & Aftermath Beat
* **Focus**: Man slumping against wall, woman resetting back into combat stance, rapid breathing.
* **Format**: Kling / Sora / Runway

```text
Medium close-up. The larger man recoils and slumps down against the wet brick wall, head hanging. The athletic woman takes half a step back, fists still raised, chest heaving with rapid ragged breaths. Her eyes remain fixed down at the opponent. Neon rim light, falling rain, grounded gritty realism.
```

## 2. Tactical Gun-Fu & Close-Quarters Disarm (John Wick Style)

### Shot 1: The Draw & Angle Shift (1.0s) — Anticipation
```text
Low-angle tight medium on a suited operative stepping through a dark doorway. His pistol is drawn and indexed at chest level, lead foot planted firmly. His eyes snap from the hallway into the room, jaw locked, silent tactical breathing. Tungsten rim lighting, cinematic anamorphic framing.
```

### Shot 2: The Deflection & Barrel Redirect (1.0s) — The Parry
```text
Over-the-shoulder close-up. The operative's left hand violently sweeps an opponent's outstretched rifle barrel upward and away from his centerline. A muzzle flash from off-screen illuminates the room for a split-second with muzzle smoke curling violently. Controlled kinetic resistance, snappy real-time speed.
```

### Shot 3: The Point-Blank Shot & Slide Recoil (1.5s) — Impact
```text
Dynamic medium close-up. The operative drives his handgun forward and fires one round off-camera left. The mechanical slide cycles with a bright brass shell casing ejecting in slow motion through a cloud of smoke. Operative's eyes stay laser-focused on target, completely devoid of emotion.
```

## 3. Medieval Sword Strike & Shield Clash

### Shot 1: The Heavy Stance & Wind-Up (1.5s)
```text
A mud-splattered armored knight shifts his entire body weight onto his rear leg, raising a broadsword with both gauntleted hands. Heavy breath plumes into the freezing morning air; mud clings to the greaves. Static 50mm framing, cool overcast lighting.
```

### Shot 2: The Downward Cleave & Shield Impact (1.0s)
```text
Fast tracking close-up. The knight's broadsword crashes violently down against an opponent's wooden round shield. Sparks and splinters burst outward upon impact; the defender's shield arm compresses downward under the massive physical weight. High-speed shutter, gritty mud splatter.
```

### Shot 3: The Shield Bash & Stumble (1.5s)
```text
The defender forcefully thrusts his shield boss forward into the knight's breastplate, causing the knight to stumble backward through the thick mud, regaining his footing on the third step. Steam rises from damp armor, realistic inertia and footing struggle.
```

## 4. Cyberpunk Kinetic Tackle Through Drywall

### Shot 1: The Sprint & Ground Drive (1.2s)
```text
Dynamic low-angle tracking shot. A cybernetic soldier charges forward at full sprint across a dim industrial hallway, boots heavily pounding the wet grating. Camera tracks backward at matching speed. Blue neon flicker, heavy motion blur along the edges.
```

### Shot 2: The Wall Breach & Debris Explosion (1.2s)
```text
[Crash push in] The cybernetic soldier slams his armored shoulder straight through an interior drywall partition. Massive white plaster dust, wooden studs, and fractured drywall chunks explode toward the camera. Extreme kinetic violence, high-speed impact physics.
```

### Shot 3: The Landing & Tactical Recovery (2.0s)
```text
Medium shot inside the adjacent room. The soldier slides across the dusty concrete floor on one knee and palm, coming to a halt as drywall dust drifts in the air. He raises his weapon, eyes scanning the room through the dust cloud.
```
